# Your first plot

[← Home](https://github.com/SyntaxDevTeam/PlotsX/blob/main/docs/wiki/en/Home.md)

Already have an idea for your base? All you need is some free land and one command.

## Claim the land

1. Find a place where the server allows plots to be created.
2. Stand where you want the center of your plot to be.
3. Run `/claim` and confirm the creation in the menu. Stay in the selected location until you confirm.
4. After the plot is created, its borders will be displayed. Creating a plot itself does not charge any fee in PlotsX.
5. Run `/plot` to open the plot panel.

In the default classic mode, a plot is **33 × 33 blocks**. In chunk mode, you claim the chunk you are standing in: **16 × 16 blocks**. Both plot types cover the full height of the world. The administrator chooses the mode, sizes, and limits.

## Invite a friend

While standing on your plot, run:

```text
/plot add Alex
```

Alex can now build together with you. If you also want to share a private chest with them, do that separately — see the [Private Chests guide](https://github.com/SyntaxDevTeam/PlotsX/blob/main/docs/wiki/en/Private-Chests.md).

## Give the base a memorable name

In `/plot`, choose the rename option. On newer servers you enter the name in a form, while older versions use chat input. You have 60 seconds to respond. Example: `Base`. You can then open its panel with `/plot Base`.

## Release the land

Stand on your own plot, run `/unclaim`, and confirm. Buildings remain in the world, but the land loses PlotsX protection. Take valuable items before removing the plot.
