# PlotsX — your place on the server

Building a house, a base with friends, or a large farm? Claim the land and set your own rules. PlotsX is a Minecraft plugin that lets players create protected plots and manage them through a convenient interface.

## Build your way

- **Your own land:** choose a place in the world and create a plot.
- **Play with friends:** add other players so you can build together.
- **Private storage:** share your containers only with selected people.
- **More space:** expand the plot when your base starts growing.
- **Your rules:** control building, containers, combat, and many other activities.
- **Convenient panel:** rename the plot, display its borders, and return to it without memorizing many commands.

PlotsX fits survival servers and shared worlds where everyone wants their own piece of land. A plot is created around the location you choose — you do not need to search for a pre-generated parcel.
